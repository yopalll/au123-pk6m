How to Recycle Your Empty Glass Pods

Our commitment to a serene, sustainable future means ensuring every beautifully crafted component finds a second life. Follow this simple guide to responsibly return your empty VIYGO glass pods and receive rewards towards your next ritual.

The Circular Ritual

keyboard_return

1. Return

Request a prepaid shipping label through your account portal. Carefully package your empty, unbroken pods in their original shipment box or any secure courier packaging.

cleaning_services

2. Sterilize

Once received at our laboratory, each pod undergoes a rigorous, hospital-grade sterilization process using sustainable, closed-loop sanitization methods to ensure absolute purity.

stars

3. Reward

As gratitude for participating in our circular economy, 500 Luxe Points will automatically be credited to your account within 48 hours of successful processing, redeemable on future curations.

Important Packaging Note

Please ensure that only glass pods are returned in this specific program. Plastic caps and applicator spatulas should be disposed of according to your local municipal recycling guidelines, as they require a different processing facility. Do not rinse the pods prior to shipping, as our facility handles all necessary preparation.
